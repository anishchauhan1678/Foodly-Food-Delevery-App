How to use a Foodly coupon

  1. 1 Choose an offer that matches your cart.
  2. 2 Copy the coupon code and keep it ready.
  3. 3 Add food to your cart before applying.
  4. 4 Enter the code in the cart and apply it.
  5. 5 Review the updated total before checkout.

Coupon details

Foodly demo coupons are frontend-only examples, designed to test cart discount logic for subtotal, percentage, and delivery-based offers.
